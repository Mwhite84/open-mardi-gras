# <Product / Area> — Roadmap

> Roadmap. Sequences what will be pursued, in what order, and why — tied to
> outcomes, not a list of features.

## Vision

The direction this roadmap serves and the outcome it works toward.

## Themes

The major areas of focus that organize the work.

## Now

What is being worked on currently, and the outcome each item targets.

## Next

What is planned to follow, and why it comes after Now.

## Later

What is on the horizon but not yet committed.

## Out of Scope

What has been considered and deliberately excluded.

## Dependencies and Risks

What this sequence depends on and what could disrupt it.
