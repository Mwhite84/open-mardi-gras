# Roadmap — Product Manager's Lens

The form of a roadmap — its sections — lives in the `doc-templates` skill
(`templates/roadmap.md`). This file covers what the **product manager** does
within that form: a roadmap sequences outcomes toward a direction, and it is a set
of deliberate choices about what *not* to do as much as what to do.

## What the PM checks

- **Items are outcomes, not just features.** Each entry names the change it
  targets, not only the thing being built.
- **The sequence is justified.** Now comes before Next for a reason — value,
  dependency, or risk — and that reason is visible.
- **It ties to a direction.** The roadmap serves a stated vision, not a pile of
  unrelated requests.
- **What is excluded is stated.** Out-of-scope and deferred items are explicit. A
  roadmap that only lists what is in is a wish list.
- **It is honest about commitment.** Later items are marked as uncommitted rather
  than implied as promises.

## Smells the PM flags

- **Wish list.** Everything requested is on it; nothing is excluded or deferred.
- **Feature factory.** A list of outputs with no outcomes attached.
- **Unsequenced.** Order with no rationale — no reason Now is Now.
- **Overcommitment.** Distant, uncertain work presented as firm commitment.
- **Directionless.** Items that serve no stated vision or theme.
